Key decisions

Three calls that shaped LOOP.

01

Warn, don't block.

Passed on hard blocks. Blocked users just open the broker and leave.

02

Reasons, not answers.

Passed on an AI chat. With money at stake, a black box is the riskiest thing a screen can show.

03

Trust before convenience.

Passed on syncing accounts. Statements are imported, and data stays on the device.

Design system

One rulebook keeps every AI-built screen consistent.

Reflection

Building with AI changed what my design job is.

  1. 01
    When building is easy, the problem is the work.

    An idea becomes a screen in an afternoon. Finding the right problem is the hard part.

  2. 02
    AI shortens every step, so set the frame first.

    Speed without a frame is drift. Set the user, the flow and the system first. Then iterate.

  3. 03
    Next, turn a tool people love into a business.

    Ten testers rely on it. The real test is whether many people will, and pay for it. That's what we're working on now.
