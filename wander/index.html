Design process

Conversation first. Screens last.

A friend can make any plan happen. An AI can’t. So I designed the behavior before the UI.

  1. 01 · Sample dialogsWrite it like a human.

    Wrote the ideal chat, the way a friend would.

  2. No idea yetHas a planNothing matchesNew in townAfter the eventHost needs people
    02 · Scenario mappingMap every situation.

    Six situations, two paths: users who need a plan, hosts who need people.

  3. 03 · User testingRead it to real users.

    Read the scripts to real users. Rewrote what felt off.

  4. 04 · Human vs AISet the rules an AI can keep.

    Real plans only. No dead ends. Remember every answer.

  5. 05 · Evals, with MLTurn it into tests.

    Every branch became a test. 54 in total, 33 had to pass to launch.

Reflection

Being the only designer meant owning the whole problem.

  1. 01
    Own the problem, not just the screens.

    At a startup, no one hands you the problem.

    Design decides what to build, not only how it looks.

  2. 02
    Design for today’s AI. Aim at tomorrow’s.

    Work within what AI can do today, or it won’t ship.

    But pick the build that still fits when AI can do it all.

  3. 03
    With AI, design is a loop, not a line.

    Direction, making and learning happen at once, and shape each other.

    The loop surfaces what needs a human: key decisions, hidden assumptions, conflicts and evidence gaps.
