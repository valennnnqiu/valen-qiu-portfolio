Research

I talked to 8 users about the feed.

Finding 01

Posts read negative. They didn't want to meet those people.

A lot of people write like they have no friends.
Finding 02

A blank box. They didn't know what to write.

I don't know what to post. This isn't Instagram. I don't know what to share.
Finding 03

Too many picks. Most didn't fit.

It shows me 20 groups every time, and they don't all fit me. I just want a few that are right.

Wander helped with one step. Users were on their own for four.

  1. ✓ WanderBrowse

    Scrolls the feed.

  2. StuckFind

    None feel right.

  3. StuckPost

    Nothing to say.

  4. StuckSay hi

    No reason to start a chat.

  5. StuckShow up

    No one to go with.

Insight

To help people meet, we had to know them better.

Scope

Six a day, not endless.

We only had about six good cards per person, per day. So I designed for scarcity, not scroll.

AI rules

Real AI broke it.
So I set the rules.

Four moments where the AI could go wrong.

  1. IfThen
  2. AI is slow (4s+)→A friendly line, not a spinner
  3. Already a match→Skip questions, show picks
  4. Need to know more→3 taps, max
  5. Nudge doesn't fit→Don't show it

Reflection

Being the only designer meant owning the whole problem.

  1. 01
    Own the problem, not just the screens.

    At a startup, no one hands you the problem.

    Design decides what to build, not only how it looks.

  2. 02
    In ambiguity, ship a bet and learn.

    There was no data and no playbook.

    A shipped answer teaches more than a perfect plan.

  3. 03
    With AI, design is a loop, not a line.

    Direction, making and learning happen at once, and shape each other.

    The loop surfaces what needs a human: key decisions, hidden assumptions, conflicts and evidence gaps.
