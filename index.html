    Rebuilding Wander's
    Post Ecosystem

    Overview

    Redesigned Wander's posting ecosystem from scratch — rebuilding onboarding and the post system to help users express themselves and create meaningful social signals.

    My role

    Product Strategy, UI, UX, User Research

    Team
    Me CEO MKT FE
    FE BE OPS
    Timeline

    2 weeks

    💡 Impact Overview
    ↑ 36.4%
    Post Usage (within 3 weeks)
    ↑ 22.3%
    Post Volume (daily prompts)
    ↑ 5.7%
    User Retention
    01

    The Challenge

    Wander successfully attracted users to join groups, but very few people were actually posting. Many users didn't know what to share, and the posting flow lacked clear guidance and social signals. This limited content creation and reduced opportunities for interaction.

    3.4%
    of users created posts — far below expectations
    70%
    of posts were text-only, lacking identity signals
    60%
    of user profiles were completely empty
    2wk
    tight timeline with no formal PRD to start from
    02

    Problem Statement

    "How might Wander help users express themselves more easily and create posts that spark conversations?"

    Milestone 1
    Establish the First Post through Onboarding
    Help Users Express Their Interests Early

    A lightweight onboarding flow that helps users quickly express their interests and create their first signals in the community.

    Milestone 2
    Redesign the Post System
    Make Posting Easy and Visible

    A posting experience with clearer entry points and prompts, guiding users to create posts and start conversations.

    03

    Process

    1. Understanding Why Users Don't Post

    I analyzed early user behavior and community activity patterns to understand why only 3.4% of users created posts.

    70%
    Text-only Post
    60%
    Profiles were Empty
    2. Defining the Strategy & Roadmap

    Based on these insights, I led cross-functional discussions and two design jams to define a clear roadmap, identifying both short- and long-term solutions.

    Short-term (2 weeks)
    Establish the first signal through onboarding
    Ensure every user creates their first post after onboarding.
    Long-term (1 month)
    Redesign the post system to drive engagement
    Introduce structured and engaging formats to encourage consistent posting.
    3. Designing onboarding for early signals

    To help users start expressing themselves, I designed a lightweight onboarding flow that captures interests and guides users to create their first post.

    Capture interests with simple questions
    Lower the barrier for users to express what they like
    Turn answers into initial signals
    Translate user inputs into visible identity signals

    Through iterative testing and validation, I refined the onboarding flow into three key steps:

    1. Find your group
    Lower the barrier for users to express what they like
    2. Start your group (optional)
    Lower the barrier for users to express what they like
    3. Create your 1st post
    Lower the barrier for users to express what they like
    4. Reshaping the Post System

    To help users continue posting, we introduced a prompt-driven system that guides users on what to share and lowers the effort to create content.

    5. Shipping Handoff through Code

    Instead of relying on traditional PRDs and handoff documents, I used vibe coding to translate design directly into working code. This allowed faster iteration, reduced communication overhead, and ensured higher design fidelity.

    04

    Design Principles

    Lower barriers. Real connection.

    Support Natural Hangouts
    Help conversations feel natural and easily translate into real-life hangouts.
    Build Authentic Community
    Turn interests into visible signals, and help users express what they're into through posts.
    Enable Effortless Expression
    Use prompts, onboarding, and lightweight interactions to help users start without overthinking.
    05

    Reflection

    Designing in an AI startup means designing, building, and validating, ALL AT ONCE!

    📣
    Design Decides What Gets Built, Not Just the UI

    Design is not only about execution — it's about deciding what should or shouldn't exist. It influences the roadmap, defines priorities, and shapes who the product is for.

    🦾
    Clarity Can Emerge Through Making

    Even without a formal PRD, real problems can be identified through use. With faster iteration enabled by AI, I can quickly prototype ideas, test flows, and use tangible outputs to align teams and drive decisions.

    🕹️
    AI Changes Speed, Not Thinking

    Vibe coding doesn't replace design thinking. For complex, ambiguous problems, structured thinking still matters. But AI becomes a powerful tool — helping me explore competitors, build flows, create micro-interactions, and deliver handoff faster.

    Improving Gauthmath
    for Better Learning

    Overview

    Gauth is ByteDance's AI-powered learning platform for North American high school students, expanding from science to humanities.

    I redesigned Gauth's humanities explanation experience by introducing Keyword Cards, a lightweight, structured way for students to grasp key concepts faster.

    My role

    Product Designer Intern

    Team
    Me PM UX Acad
    FE FE BE BE
    Timeline

    5 weeks · Launched on time

    💡Impact Overview
    ↑ 7.3%
    User Retention (Humanities)
    ↓ 6.2%
    Negative Feedback
    5 wks
    Shipped on Time
    01

    The Challenge

    The platform needed a clearer way to support learning beyond showing answers. Students struggled to extract key ideas from long explanations, contributing to lower retention and higher negative feedback in these subjects.

    5 wks
    Timeline to expand into humanities
    0
    Existing user research to guide decisions
    6
    Proposed features lacking clear prioritization
    02

    Problem Statement

    "Gauth's AI explanations were strong in science but failed to deliver clear, structured learning support in humanities."

    Milestone 1
    Keyword Card
    Structured Learning at a Glance

    A clean, expandable card that summarizes definitions, key points, and sub-topics, helping students grasp concepts faster.

    Milestone 2
    Mind Map
    Visualizing How Knowledge Connects

    A simplified mobile-friendly mind map that maintains structure without overwhelming users.

    Milestone 3
    Gauthpedia & One-page Summary
    Fast Entry to Deeper Learning

    A redesigned article entry card that improves readability, hierarchy, and engagement.

    03

    Process

    1. Understanding the Learning Journey

    I mapped the student journey for humanities questions to identify where explanations broke down. Two key scenarios emerged:

    Daily learning
    Students needed quick, clear concept understanding.
    Exam review
    Students needed to connect related ideas efficiently.
    2. Prioritizing What Matters

    The PM proposed six potential features. Through user research, competitive analysis, and my prior experience, I helped narrow focus to the Keyword Card as the first milestone, the most feasible and high-impact feature for improving humanities learning.

    3. Designing for Clarity and Structure

    I explored variations in placement, content structure, and interactions. Early versions were either too minimal or too heavy.

    Through iterative testing and academic validation, I shaped a card system that:

    Highlights essential concepts
    Clear structure for fast understanding.
    Expands cleanly into details
    Smooth transition from summary to depth.
    Adapts across subjects and AI outputs
    Consistent experience across all content types.
    4. Scaling Across Related Features

    With the core structure defined, we extended it to related modules. These features now form a coherent learning ecosystem anchored by the Keyword Card.

    Mind Map
    Simplifying complex knowledge graphs for mobile.
    Gauthpedia & One-page Summary
    Improving readability, hierarchy, and entry points.
    5. Establishing an AI Output Guideline

    Created a clear handoff guide, defining structure, length, and image rules, so AI-generated content stays predictable, consistent, and aligned with the design system.

    04

    Design Principles

    Clear structure. Less effort. Better learning.

    Make Concepts Scannable
    Break long explanations into clear, structured components so students can grasp key ideas in seconds.
    Reduce Cognitive Load
    Design lightweight, expandable interactions that give students only what they need at each step.
    Build for a System, Not a Single Feature
    Create UI patterns flexible enough to scale across humanities content — Keyword Cards, Mind Map, and Gauthpedia all follow the same logic.
    05

    Reflection

    Designing for AI is designing the future; intuitive, adaptive, and built for the next generation.

    💪
    Embracing AI's Limits & Possibilities

    I learned to balance feasibility with innovation, working within AI's constraints while still pushing for clearer, more structured learning experiences.

    🔍
    Focusing on What Truly Matters

    Not every problem needs AI. Prioritizing high-value use cases helped ensure AI improved learning rather than adding complexity.

    ✨
    Adapting to New Interaction

    GPT reshaped user expectations. This project pushed me to rethink how users ask, search, and interact with AI in a more intuitive way.

    📊
    Designing with Data & Insights

    Grounding design decisions in user behavior and competitive insights reinforced that strong design must be both strategic and evidence-based.

    Creating Financial Journeys
    for Everyday Banking

    Overview

    A collaboration between OCAD University's Design4 program and Scotiabank's Digital Product team. I led research and design exploration for an adaptive goal-setting experience — rethinking how users define, track, and connect financial goals with banking products.

    My role

    Product Designer Intern

    Team
    Me Ren Pei Lau
    Kevin Brian
    Timeline

    6 months

    💡Impact Overview
    2
    High-Value AI Concepts
    60+
    Signals in Trend Report
    01

    The Challenge

    Scotiabank's financial goal-setting and daily banking flows were fragmented, slow, and hard to navigate. Young users faced long steps, unclear actions, and weak connections between goals, insights, and real financial products — leading to low engagement and low trust.

    We needed to understand and integrate emerging technologies such as AI and voice to help Scotiabank shape a smarter, more adaptive banking journey over the next 10 years.

    01
    Vision-driven project required a speculative research framework
    02
    No clear scope on problems, scenarios, and tech
    03
    Align emerging tech with user needs to shape a 10-year vision
    02

    Problem Statement

    "How might Scotiabank use AI to create a personalized and adaptive banking experience for the young users in 2035?"

    Concept 1

    Adaptive Content and UI

    Effortless Everyday Banking

    AI learns users habits and automates routine steps, creating a smooth, intuitive experience for daily transactions.

    Personalized Goal Setting

    Helps users set goals with a dedicated saving account, making long-term goals easier to start and achieve.

    Track Progress Anywhere

    Real-time updates keep users motivated and tracked inside and outside the app.

    Concept 2

    AI-Powered Personal Banking Assistant

    Ubiquitous Conversational Banking

    Access banking assistant anywhere, through chat or voice, for quick questions and instant actions.

    Intelligent Suggested Actions

    Based on user context, AI predicts next steps, making banking feel effortless.

    03

    Process

    1. Setting a Future-Focused Research Framework

    We built a speculative research approach combining future trend scanning and user interviews to explore how technology, economics, banking, and user behavior may evolve over the next decade, while keeping the vision grounded in real needs.

    60+
    Signals
    11
    Trends
    Using a macro-to-micro speculative design lens, we identified the key signals shaping the next 10 years of personal finance.
    2. Defining Opportunity Spaces & Priorities

    Through workshops, competitive reviews, and alignment with product teams, we focused on two high-value scenarios that best reflect the potential of personalization and AI-assisted banking:

    Adaptive Content and UI
    Designing a system that responds intelligently to user intent, context, and behavior.
    AI-Powered Personal Banking Assistant
    Supporting users with meaningful guidance, not just tasks, across their financial journey.
    This impact-effort matrix helped us identify the most promising future scenarios by evaluating feasibility against user and business value.
    3. Understanding Young Users' Personalized Needs

    Through 11 interviews, we identified two main young user types: local students and international students. While their situations differ, their expectations for personalized, simplified, and guided banking were highly consistent.

    11
    Interviews
    120+
    Data
    2
    Archetypes
    14
    Insights

    Through synthesis and workshops, we narrowed the focus to the two most impactful directions:

    Helping Users Set & Track Any Goal
    Enable flexible goal creation that adapts to intentions, not rigid financial product categories.
    Boosting Daily Transaction Efficiency
    Reduce cognitive load and steps while maintaining clarity, transparency, and trust.
    4. Reimagining Key Scenarios to Showcase AI's Potential

    We selected the scenarios with the highest long-term value and redesigned them to demonstrate how adaptive intelligence can simplify decisions, reduce friction, and create a more intuitive banking journey.

    Concept 1
    Adaptive Content and UI
    Imagine a banking app that adapts to your financial habits, offering smart shortcuts and a tailored experience at your fingertips.
    Customisable UI
    An experience unique to you.
    Concept 2
    AI-Powered Personal Banking Assistant
    Imagine a banking experience tailored to your unique needs, with an all-in-one virtual assistant in your pocket for seamless chat and voice banking, accessible anytime, anywhere.
    Your personal advisor
    5. Mapping Where Complexity Creates Friction

    By deeply understanding how different banking products work and how users move through, we uncovered where complexity creates friction across key flows. This allowed us to balance business needs with a lightweight, intuitive, happy-path experience.

    6. Delivering a Future-Ready UI Library

    We evolved the existing Scotia design system into a future-ready asset library, introducing new visual rules, interaction patterns, and AI-specific components to improve clarity, trust, and transparency.

    04

    Design Principles

    Simpler banking. Relevant actions. Intelligent support.

    Reduce Complexity at Every Step
    Simplify flows by minimizing decision points and removing unnecessary cognitive load.
    Personalization With Purpose
    Use AI to deliver timely, contextual guidance that clarifies next steps and increases trust.
    Build a Cohesive, Adaptive Experience
    Content, UI, and AI insights scale consistently across scenarios, growing with the user.
    05

    Reflection

    Designing future banking is about making complexity simple, guidance meaningful, and every experience feel genuinely human.

    🎯
    Focusing on High-Value User Scenarios

    It's crucial to identify where AI can genuinely improve everyday banking instead of adding complexity. Designing for the overall experience, not a single function.

    🖋️
    Sharpening Product Sense

    Understanding how different banking products work helped me see where complexity creates friction — and how to balance business needs with a simpler path.

    🫱🏻‍🫲🏾
    Driving Accountability and Team Ownership

    Working across designers and stakeholders taught me to communicate clearly, align early, and take ownership to keep the project moving forward.
