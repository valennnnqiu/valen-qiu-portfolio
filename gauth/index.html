Research

I interviewed 3 long-term users about how they study.

Finding 01

They read for the key point, then leave.

Finding 02

To remember, they copy it into a notebook.

Finding 03

Before exams, they reconnect topics into one picture.

Insight

Students don't need more answers. They need to understand, then remember.

Which best describes the main cause of World War II?

Answer

C. The rivalry between totalitarian expansionist powers in Europe and those in Asia

Explanation

  1. Authoritarian leaders rose in countries hit by the Great Depression.
  2. Germany, Italy and Japan pursued aggressive expansion.
  3. Appeasement failed to stop them.

The most significant factor was the rise of authoritarian leaders, which led to aggressive expansionist policies in Europe and Asia.

AI content rules

Real AI output broke it.
So I designed the output.

With the academic lead and prompt engineer, I wrote these limits into the prompt.

01

Too much text? Cap every field.

Main content1–5 lines · ~25–120 chars
02

Too many points? Three at most.

Reflection

Designing for AI starts before the screen.

  1. 01
    In AI products, the output is part of the design.

    A perfect mockup means nothing if the model can't produce it.

  2. 02
    Put AI where it matters most, not everywhere.

    AI is costly. Research showed it helps most with understanding and remembering.

  3. 03
    Let data turn opinions into a roadmap.

    Complaint data and interviews, not opinions, cut two features.
