Finding 02

AI content rules

Real AI output broke it.
So I designed the output.

With the academic lead and prompt engineer, I wrote these limits into the prompt.

01

Too much text? Cap every field.

Main content1–5 lines · ~25–120 chars
02

Too many points? Three at most.

Key pointsup to 3
03

Different subjects? One template each.

Reflection

Designing for AI starts before the screen.

  1. 01
    Embracing AI’s Limits & Possibilities

    I learned to balance feasibility with innovation, working within AI’s constraints while still pushing for clearer, more structured learning experiences.

  2. 02
    Focusing on What Truly Matters

    Not every problem needs AI. Prioritizing high-value use cases helped ensure AI improved learning rather than adding complexity.

  3. 03
    Adapting to New Interaction

    GPT reshaped user expectations. This project pushed me to rethink how user ask, search, and interact with AI in a more intuitive way.

  4. 04
    Designing with Data & Insights

    Grounding design decisions in user behavior and competitive insights reinforced that strong design must be both strategic and evidence-based.
