Challenge

Design banking for 2035. No scope given.

Scotiabank asked how emerging tech like AI and voice could shape the next ten years of banking for young users.

01 Speculative brief

A vision, not a backlog.

A ten-year horizon needed a research method first.

02 Nothing scoped

Every variable was open.

Problems, scenarios and tech were ours to define.

03 Friction today

Goals felt far from products.

Long flows left goals disconnected from real products.

How might we

How might AI make Scotiabank personal and adaptive for young users in 2035?

Research

I built the framework before anyone drew a screen.

Future signals and real interviews, read side by side, so the 2035 vision stayed tied to what young users need now.

60+Future signals
12User interviews
14Insights
2Archetypes

Macro to micro, in four steps.

  1. Step 1Trend scanning

    60+ signals across tech, banking and behavior, clustered into 11 trends.

  2. Step 2Ground it in users

    12 interviews with young users. A trend only stayed if it answered a need we heard.

  3. Step 3Synthesis

    Interview data into 2 archetypes and 14 insights.

  4. Step 4Two scenarios, not a vision deck

    Workshops scored every idea on personalization and sustainability. Two cleared both bars.

Two kinds of young users. One ask.

Different starting points, the same expectation: banking that's personal, simple, and guided.

  • Archetype 01 · International studentNew to Canada, new to its banks.

    Overwhelmed by unfamiliar processes and products.

  • Archetype 02 · Local studentDoesn't save, doesn't track.

    Sees long-term goals as out of reach.

Insight

Fourteen insights. Two changed the design.

  1. Insight 01 Young users think in goals, not products.

    So a new goal starts from what you're working towards.

  2. Insight 02 Personalization meant easier transfers.

    Not smarter investments, as we'd assumed. So a transfer takes 5 steps, not 8.

Key calls

Three calls that kept the vision buildable.

Call 01

Steps over a single page.

BEFORE

One form with every setting. Fewer clicks, not easier.

AFTER

Three steps: name it, set details, personalize.

Call 02

A savings account before GICs.

BEFORE

Goals tied to GICs and funds. Complex, hard to trust.

AFTER

A savings account first. More behind "Learn more".

Call 03

Merge the flows, keep the confirmation.

BEFORE

6 steps, no confirmation. High risk, too black-box.

AFTER

5 steps. Suggest and pre-fill, you still confirm.

Design system

Reflection

Future work matters when part of it lands today.

  1. 01
    Frame the future with real people.

    Trends show what's possible. Interviews show what's worth building. Pairing them is why one concept made it into the app.

  2. 02
    Lead before the title.

    As the intern team's lead, I set the framework, split the work, and kept us aligned with Scotiabank's designers from first workshop to final pitch.

  3. 03
    In money, trust beats speed.

    AI only helps if people can see why it suggests something. It's the same rule behind "reasons, not answers" in LOOP.
